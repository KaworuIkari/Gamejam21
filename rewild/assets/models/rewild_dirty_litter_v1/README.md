# Dirty damaged litter

Separate pack with eight damaged objects and three messy piles. Includes flattened can, collapsed uncapped bottle with detached cap, stained squashed cup, torn carton, shredded dirty bag, filthy tray, torn foil wrapper, and open rusted food tin.

Dirtier silhouettes and materials: warped packaging, open/torn edges, peeling labels, grease/mildew patches, rust and loose fragments. These are stylized low-poly material/geometry details, not photorealistic texture scans. The clean litter pack is preserved.

Open sample.tscn to see actual meter scale. The contact sheet enlarges small individual pieces for comparison. Drag the individual scenes into your level. Each includes Clearable metadata and a ClearTarget Area3D. The target has collision layer/mask zero until assigned to your game's interaction layers. No solid player-blocking collision is used.

Clearing, task counts, hold input, saves and bloom must connect to your existing gameplay system. Mixed heaps are one target each. Geometry is static and has no physics simulation. No existing models or playable scenes were replaced.
