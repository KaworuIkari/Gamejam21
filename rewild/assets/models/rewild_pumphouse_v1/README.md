# Ruined northern pump house

Large permanent waterworks landmark with a 16×12m foundation and roughly 14m roof height. Includes exposed steel roof trusses, broken walls and rebar, a large pump and motor, intake pipe, maintenance gantry, control cabinet and hanging cables.

Three matching scenes: Wasteland, Revival, Golden. The structure, origin and collision remain identical; vegetation increases from sparse new growth to longer vines and shrubs. Golden-hour lighting comes from your WorldStages environment, not baked colors.

Place near the northern spring and combine with existing Spring_Intake and clearable rubble assets. The pump house is a background/structural landmark, not a new task gate. The machine and control cabinet are visual props without repair interactions. The gantry is not a tested player route.

Use `sample.tscn` for the comparison, or drag an individual stage scene into the map. Static collision layer 1 covers structure only. No stage switching or level placement is performed automatically; select/swap variants using the existing WorldStages.stage_changed index 0/1/2.
