Wake up on an island the world forgot.

A plane lies broken on the sand. The pier is rusting, the radar is silent and the lake station has gone dark. You are a small water robot with a hose, a tank and a lot of time.

Clean the land, restore the water and bring the power back, and watch every patch of grass you touch spring back to life. Then find out what this island has been waiting for.

What you do

Small hands, a big island

A cosy, hand-drawn exploration game about leaving a place better than you found it.

Rewild the land

Spray away the grime and watch grass, flowers and trees grow back across every region.

Restore the island

Reopen the spring, restart the lake station and wake the sleeping machines that once kept the island running.

Gear up

Upgrade your water tank, earn a jetpack and reach the corners of the island you couldn’t before.

The journey

From a wreck to a way home

The crash site

Start among the wreckage and work out what happened to this place.

Restore the water

Get the north spring flowing again, then head to the central island and reboot the lake power station.

Wake the energy core

Collect scattered gears in the abandoned factory and repair a sleeping little bot.

Restart the radar

Revive the mountains, use your key on the radar station and read the coordinates it has been holding.

The last journey

Power up the Chaos Energy Module and bring everything you’ve restored together for the island’s final chapter.

Download for Mac
First launch help

Open the .dmg and drag Rewild into Applications. The build is not notarized, so macOS blocks it the first time. Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Rewild.

Windows

Your system
Zip archive · Windows 10/11, 64-bit · ~1.4 GB unpacked
Download for Windows
First launch help

Unzip the whole folder and run rewild.exe. Keep the .dll files next to the exe. If SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.
